Each maneuver stops collecting as soon as enough steady-speed data is available, then retraces its path and stops. Forward/backward can travel up to 6 m or 12 seconds; turns can extend beyond 1 m, up to 3 m or 12 seconds. Single-check mode waits for your confirmation between maneuvers; Run all advances automatically. Maximum turn means the vehicle’s current steering limit. LiDAR checks the next 20 cm of travel beyond the vehicle footprint, with a 20 cm side margin.

        
Prepare an open test area
Keep at least 6.5 m clear ahead and behind, and 3.5 m on each side, measured from the starting vehicle footprint. Use a flat surface with good LiDAR visibility. Stay with the vehicle. The confirmed maneuver and return continue if you switch windows, close the browser, or lose its connection; single-check mode waits afterward; Run all continues automatically. Use Stop calibration or E-stop to stop it.
